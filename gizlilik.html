Böcekcan & Diva Privacy Policy

46RUK Studios · Effective date: November 2, 2026

Böcekcan & Diva: Diva'yı Kurtar is a mobile game developed by 46RUK Studios. In short: the game does not collect any personal data.

Information collected

The game requires no account, sign-up or login. It does not access or collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Information stored on your device

Your high score, sound on/off preference and whether you have watched the opening story are stored only on your device. They are never sent to any server and are removed when you delete the game.

Internet, ads and analytics

The game works offline and sends no data to any server. It contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party data collection tools, and no in-app purchases.

Children

The game collects no personal data from any user, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
